Create customized savings goals with automated simulation, risk profiling, and 100% online tracking. 1. Before You Start

Verify your active Member ID, updated contact email, and registered bank or HealthyPay account in My Portal.

2. Digital Processing

Submit your request or supporting documents online via Virtual Branch or the More Healthy App with biometric validation.

Receive instant SMS/email confirmation and direct deposit into your HealthyPay or personal bank account.
